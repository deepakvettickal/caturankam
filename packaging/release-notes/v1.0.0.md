The first release of **Caturankam**: a super-lightweight native Mac chess client for
Lichess. No browser engine, no Electron: about 66 MB of memory and 0% CPU when idle.

## Highlights

- **Play on Lichess:** log in with your account, then quick pairing (Rapid and Classical)
  or the Lichess computer at levels 1–8, including Blitz.
- **Every Lichess piece set and board**, downloaded when you pick one.
- **A proper Mac app:** menu bar, light and dark appearance, a collapsible sidebar, and a
  keyboard shortcut for every action.

## Install

Download `Caturankam-1.0.0.dmg` below, open it, and drag **Caturankam** to
**Applications**. It runs on macOS 11 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel.

**First launch:** this release isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the
first time. Open it once, choose **Done**, then go to **System Settings ▸ Privacy &
Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Caturankam, then confirm with
your password. You only do this once. macOS may also ask to let Caturankam use your Keychain, for your Lichess login;
choose **Always Allow**.

To check the download: `shasum -a 256 Caturankam-1.0.0.dmg` should match
`checksums.txt`.

## Good to know

- Lichess only lets apps like this one pair you at Rapid and Classical. Blitz works
  against the computer, and Bullet isn't available to third-party apps.
- Caturankam is an independent project, not an official Lichess app.
