A small update to **Caturankam** with a new app icon: a rook, drawn as a pencil
construction sketch on graph paper. At the smallest sizes it becomes a plain silhouette
so it stays sharp in Finder and menus.

## Install

Download `Caturankam-1.0.1.dmg` below, open it, and drag **Caturankam** to
**Applications**, replacing the old version. With Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask caturankam`.

**First launch:** this release isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the
first time. Open it once, choose **Done**, then go to **System Settings ▸ Privacy &
Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Caturankam. macOS may ask again to let
Caturankam use your Keychain; choose **Always Allow**.

If Finder or the Dock still shows the old icon, it's macOS's icon cache: it catches up
after a restart of the Dock (`killall Dock`) or of the Mac.

To check the download: `shasum -a 256 Caturankam-1.0.1.dmg` should match
`checksums.txt`.
